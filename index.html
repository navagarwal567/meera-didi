Dear Meera Didi,

I'm writing this because a simple “sorry” sometimes doesn't feel like enough.

I know I've made mistakes, and I know there may have been moments when my words or actions hurt you. For those moments, I'm genuinely sorry. I don't want to make excuses for them. I just want to accept that I could have handled things better.

You've always been someone special to me, and I don't want a temporary misunderstanding or one bad moment to become bigger than the bond we share.

On this Raksha Bandhan, I also want to say thank you. Thank you for your care, your support, your advice, and for all the little things you have done that may have seemed ordinary but meant a lot to me.

I may not always express it properly, but having you as my sister is something I genuinely value.

So today, along with wishing you a very Happy Raksha Bandhan, I want to say one more thing:

I'm really sorry, Didi. ❤️

I hope we can leave the hurt behind, keep the good memories close, and continue being there for each other.
